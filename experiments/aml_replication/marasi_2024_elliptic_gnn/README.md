\# Marasi \& Ferretti 2024 Elliptic GNN Replication



\## Purpose



Replicate the Elliptic GNN comparison paper.



\## Paper



Marasi \& Ferretti (2024), Anti-Money Laundering in Cryptocurrencies Through Graph Neural Networks: A Comparative Study.



\## Dataset



Elliptic Bitcoin transaction dataset.



\## Models



\- GCN

\- GAT

\- GATv2

\- ChebNet

\- GraphSAGE



\## Original setup



\- Split: 65/15/20 train/validation/test

\- Label proportions maintained

\- Task: illicit vs licit transaction classification



\## Reported illicit F1



\- GCN: 0.616

\- GAT: 0.766

\- GraphSAGE: 0.889

\- ChebNet: 0.910

\- GATv2: 0.881
